# K G1 Safe Short Response Diagnostic v0.6.8.2

Run once with Cyan force-stopped. Do not take a photo.

The app performs one media-count query, one P2P ENTER, 20 seconds maximum credential observation, then one EXIT. It has no Wi-Fi Direct, HTTP, media-transfer, import, or ledger code.

Only valid `0x41` payloads shorter than 8 bytes may be shown in hex. These are structurally too short to contain credentials under the established parser.
